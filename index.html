NUTRITIVE AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide including name, email, age, weight, height, and health goals to personalize your nutrition experience.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide AI-powered nutrition tracking, meal analysis, diet planning, and personalized health recommendations.

3. Firebase & Google Services

We use Firebase for authentication and push notifications. Google's privacy policy applies to these services.

4. AI & Camera

Food photos are processed by Gemini AI for nutrition analysis. Photos are uploaded to Cloudinary for storage.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored securely in MongoDB. We use JWT authentication and HTTPS encryption for all communications.

6. Third Party Services

We use: Firebase (Google), Cloudinary, Gemini AI (Google), Open Food Facts API.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for users 13 years and older.

8. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data by contacting us at thiruvaliaalvan@gmail.com

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. You can request deletion of your personal data by contacting thiruvaliaalvan@gmail.com. You can also delete your account through the app, where available. When an account is deleted, we delete the user account and associated records handled by our account deletion process. Some data held by third-party services, backups, or logs may require additional time to be removed or may be retained where required by law.
