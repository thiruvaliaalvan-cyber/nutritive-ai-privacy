NUTRITIVE AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

NUTRITIVE AI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Account and profile: name, email, age, gender, weight, height, goals, diet preferences, allergies
  • Health and nutrition data: meals you log, calories, nutrients, water, body measurements
  • Health Connect (only if you allow it): steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, calories burned, sleep, distance and exercise sessions, read from Android Health Connect
  • Photos: food photos you scan and body transformation photos you choose to upload
  • Voice and chat: messages, and voice you record, when you use Twin AI or the AI nutritionist. Voice recordings are sent only to be turned into text, and we do not store them.
  • Menstrual cycle data (only if you choose to track it)
  • Approximate location (only if you allow it): used only to show local weather
  • Device and usage information: push notification (FCM) token, advertising ID on the free plan
  • Purchase information: your subscription status and a Google Play purchase token, so we can verify your plan. We never see your card or payment details; Google Play processes payments.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • To provide personalized nutrition tracking, diet plans and AI recommendations
  • To analyze food photos and answer your questions with AI
  • To show your activity, sleep and heart-rate information from Health Connect inside the app
  • To show local weather
  • To send notifications such as meal reminders and health alerts
  • To verify and manage your subscription
  • To show ads to users on the free plan
  • To keep the app secure and improve it

3. HEALTH CONNECT DATA

  • We only read the data types listed above, and only after you give permission. You can withdraw permission at any time in Health Connect or your phone settings.
  • Health Connect data is used only to give you features inside this app.
  • We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with advertisers.

4. ADVERTISING

  • Users on the free plan see ads provided by Google AdMob. Pro and Pro Max plans have no ads.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • Google may use your device's advertising ID to show ads. You can reset or opt out of it in your Android settings.

5. SERVICES WE USE

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and push notifications) and Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Billing (subscriptions) and Google AdMob (ads)
  • Cloud hosting and database (MongoDB Atlas) and Cloudinary (photo storage)
  • Google Gemini, the AI that analyzes your food photos and chat messages to produce answers
  • OpenAI Whisper, which converts voice messages you record into text, and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, which reads AI replies aloud
  • OpenWeather, which receives your approximate coordinates to return local weather

These providers process data to deliver their service and are bound by their own privacy terms.

6. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

  • Your data is stored on secure cloud servers and photos use secure links
  • Data is encrypted in transit, and we protect sensitive data with encryption
  • We keep your data while your account is active
  • Some data in backups or logs may take longer to be removed, or may be kept where the law requires

7. DATA SHARING

  • We do NOT sell your personal data
  • We do NOT share your health data with advertisers
  • We may use anonymized data to improve our AI models
  • We may share data if required by law

8. AI AND HEALTH DISCLAIMER

  • Answers and estimates (including calories and nutrients) are generated by AI and can be wrong
  • NUTRITIVE AI is not medical advice. Consult a doctor or registered dietitian for health concerns
  • You can report a problem AI response with the flag button in the chat

9. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

  • You can view and edit your data in the app
  • You can delete your account and data at any time: Profile > Privacy & Security > Delete My Account. This permanently removes your profile and associated data, such as meal logs, photos, cycle data, diet plans and your sign-in account, from our systems.
  • You can also request deletion by emailing thiruvaliaalvan@gmail.com
  • You can turn off notifications, location, microphone, camera and Health Connect access in your phone settings

10. CHILDREN

NUTRITIVE AI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

We may update this policy. We will change the date above when we do.
